      Reference equations. GLI Global (race-neutral; Bowerman et al., AJRCCM 2023) for FEV₁, FVC and FEV₁/FVC — the default on the GLI calculator and recommended by the ATS (2023). GLI-2012 (Quanjer et al., ERJ 2012) is also available and adds FEF₂₅₋₇₅. Calculations use the published LMS coefficients and spline lookup tables, interpolated linearly by age, so they reproduce the calculator to rounding.

      Normal range. The lower limit of normal (LLN) is the 5th percentile of healthy people of the same age, height and sex (z-score −1.645), as defined in the ERS/ATS 2022 interpretation standard. “Below normal range” means a value is under this limit; it is not a diagnosis.

      This app is for tracking results over time. It does not interpret results or diagnose any condition. Please discuss your results with your care team.
